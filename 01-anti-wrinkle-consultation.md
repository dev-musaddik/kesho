# Anti-Wrinkle Treatments in North London: What to Expect from Your First Consultation

If you have been thinking about anti-wrinkle treatment for a while, the consultation is almost certainly the bit you are most nervous about. You are not alone — most patients who walk into our Finchley clinic for the first time tell us they have been reading about treatments for months before booking.

This guide walks through what happens at an anti-wrinkle consultation north London patients can actually trust: who you meet, what we ask, what we assess, and why it often matters more than the treatment itself.

## Why the first consultation matters more than the treatment itself

The injection takes around ten minutes. The thinking that goes into it takes much longer.

A well-run consultation is where the result is actually decided — where we work out which muscles are doing what on your face, how strong they are, what you actually dislike when you look in the mirror, and whether anti-wrinkle treatment is the right answer at all. Get this right and the treatment is straightforward. Skip it, as some clinics effectively do, and you end up with results that look "done", asymmetric, or simply disappointing.

At KESHO, the consultation is complimentary and entirely separate from any decision to treat. You can walk out without booking anything, and a fair number of people do. That is by design.

## What happens before you arrive

Once you book, you will receive a confirmation email with a short medical intake form to complete before your appointment. It covers general health (particularly anything neuromuscular, autoimmune, or affecting clotting), current medications and supplements (including blood thinners and high-dose fish oils), allergies, pregnancy and breastfeeding status (we do not treat in either case), and previous aesthetic treatments.

We also ask what you are hoping to address and, importantly, what you are hoping to *avoid* looking like. The latter is often more telling than the former.

A few practical tips: come with a clean face if you can, avoid alcohol the night before (it can increase bruising risk if you are treated the same day), and bring a list of questions. No question is too basic.

## The medical assessment and why a prescriber is essential

Here is something a surprising number of people do not know: **botulinum toxin is a Prescription-Only Medicine (POM) in the UK.** It cannot legally be prescribed by a beauty therapist, an aesthetician without prescribing rights, or anyone who has done a short course. It must be prescribed by a doctor, dentist, pharmacist independent prescriber, or nurse independent prescriber.

Plenty of UK clinics get around this by using a "remote prescriber" — a prescriber who has never met you, signs off the prescription based on a form, and is paid per signature. It is widely considered poor practice by every serious professional body in the field.

At KESHO, your prescriber meets you in person. **Kyle**, our Independent Prescribing Pharmacist (GPhC-registered), reviews your medical history, confirms there are no contraindications, discusses the specific product and dose, and writes your prescription personally. If anything in your history needs a second medical opinion, **Dr Ryan Marni**, our GMC-registered doctor, is part of the team and available.

This is not a marketing point — it is the standard the industry is supposed to operate at, and one of the main reasons our [anti-wrinkle treatments](/treatments/anti-wrinkle/) sit at a different price point to high-street offers. You are paying for the assessment, the prescriber, and the accountability.

## Discussing your goals and why we sometimes recommend NOT treating

This is the part of the consultation patients tend to remember most.

We talk through what is realistically achievable in *your* face, not a generic one. Deep, etched static lines visible at rest will soften but not vanish; we will be honest about that and discuss whether skin treatments or a combined plan makes sense over time. If your forehead lines are mostly compensating for slightly heavy brows, treating the forehead in isolation can drop them further — so we might recommend treating only the frown, or not treating at all that day.

We have turned away patients with unrealistic expectations, those within two weeks of a major event (results take time to settle), those who recently had treatment elsewhere that needs to wear off first, and those who seemed to be making the decision under pressure. A clinic that treats every person who walks in is not a clinic with standards — it is a sales floor.

## Your first treatment day: what to expect

Many patients choose to be treated on the same day as the consultation; others prefer to go home and think about it. Both are fine.

If you proceed:

1. **Photos and markings.** We re-photograph and mark your injection points with a removable pencil, asking you to make the relevant expressions again so the dose is matched to *your* muscle activity.
2. **The injections.** A series of very fine needle pricks, usually four to six per area. Most patients describe it as a brief sting or pinch. The whole process takes five to ten minutes.
3. **Immediate aftercare.** You may have small raised bumps (like nettle stings) that settle within an hour, occasionally a small bruise.

You will not look different walking out. **Results typically appear from day 3, with the full effect visible by day 14** — which is why we book your review at two weeks, not two days.

## Aftercare and follow-up

For the first 24 hours we ask you to avoid rubbing or massaging the treated areas, stay upright for around four hours (no lying flat or face-down massages), skip strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and very hot showers, and avoid alcohol where possible. Avoid facials, microneedling and other facial treatments for two weeks.

At your **two-week review** — included in your treatment fee — we reassess the result together. If a small top-up is clinically appropriate (occasionally one muscle responds more strongly than another), it is done then at no extra charge. Results typically last **3 to 4 months**, though this can vary between 2 and 6 months depending on the individual, the area, and how active the muscles are.

## Common questions answered

**Will it hurt?**
Most patients are surprised by how little they feel. The needles are very fine, and we can apply topical numbing cream if you are anxious — though most do not need it.

**Will I look "frozen"?**
Not with us. Our default is a natural, expressive result — softened lines, full movement. If you specifically want a more "set" look we can discuss it, but we will never default to it.

**Is it safe?**
Botulinum toxin has been used clinically for over thirty years and is one of the most studied medicines in aesthetics. Risks are low when treatment is properly assessed, prescribed and administered — which is exactly what our process is designed to ensure.

**What if I do not like it?**
The effect wears off gradually over a few months — it is not permanent. At your two-week review we can refine dose and placement for next time.

**Can I have treatment if I am breastfeeding or trying to conceive?**
We do not treat during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you are actively trying to conceive, we will have an open conversation about timing.

**How is this different from a cheaper deal I have seen on Instagram?**
The big differences are usually who prescribes (in person vs remote), who injects (regulated practitioner vs short-course trained), the product used (licensed vs grey-market), and the follow-up. Price is downstream of all of that.
