## 9. TREATMENT PAGE — Anti-Wrinkle (`/treatments/anti-wrinkle/`)

### Hero
- **Eyebrow:** TREATMENT
- **H1:** Anti-Wrinkle Treatment
- **Subtext:** Smooth fine lines, refresh your appearance. Expertly-administered botulinum toxin treatments — tailored to natural-looking results.
- **Price anchor:** From £180
- **CTA:** Book Consultation → `/book/`

### What It Is
**H2:** What is anti-wrinkle treatment?
Anti-wrinkle treatment uses small, precise injections of botulinum toxin to relax the muscles responsible for expression lines. By calming these muscles, the skin above smooths, lines soften, and the face appears more rested — without losing your ability to express yourself naturally.

Botulinum toxin is a Prescription-Only Medicine in the UK. At KESHO, all treatments are prescribed and administered by our in-house team — never via remote prescription.

### Who It's For
- People in their late 20s onwards who are noticing dynamic lines (those that appear with expression)
- Those bothered by frown lines, forehead lines, or crow's feet
- Anyone considering preventative treatment to slow the formation of deeper lines
- People who want subtle, natural results — not a frozen face

### The KESHO Approach
3-card section:
- **Consultation-first.** Every treatment begins with a thorough assessment of your facial anatomy, expressions, and goals.
- **Premium products only.** We use trusted, regulated botulinum toxin brands — never compromise on quality.
- **Movement preserved.** We treat conservatively to soften lines while keeping your face expressive and natural.

*"Areas" refers to: forehead lines, frown lines (between brows), crow's feet (around eyes).*

### What to Expect (Timeline)
1. **Consultation (30-45 min)** — assessment, plan, prescription
2. **Treatment (15-20 min)** — quick, minimal discomfort
3. **Recovery (24 hours)** — avoid exercise, lying flat, alcohol
4. **Results (3-14 days)** — peaks at 2 weeks, complimentary review

### Aftercare
**Do:**
- Stay upright for 4 hours after treatment
- Use cold compresses if any swelling
- Continue gentle skincare

**Don't:**
- Exercise vigorously for 24 hours
- Lie flat or massage the treated area for 4 hours
- Drink alcohol for 24 hours
- Have facials or saunas for 48 hours

### FAQs
- **How long do results last?** Typically 3-4 months, sometimes longer with regular treatment.
- **Does it hurt?** A very fine needle is used; most clients describe it as a brief pinch.
- **When will I see results?** Subtle effects from day 3; full results at 10-14 days.
- **Is it safe?** When prescribed and administered by registered practitioners, yes. Our in-house pharmacist prescribes every treatment.
- **What if I don't like it?** Botulinum toxin gradually wears off over months — effects aren't permanent.
- **Can I have it before an event?** Yes, but book at least 2 weeks ahead.

### Hero
- **H1:** Lip Filler Treatment
- **Subtext:** Subtle volume, balanced shape, natural movement. Premium hyaluronic acid fillers — administered with restraint.
- **Price:** From £150

### What It Is
Lip filler uses hyaluronic acid — a substance your body produces naturally — to add subtle volume, balance shape, or define the lip border. At KESHO, our philosophy is restraint: filler should enhance, never overpower.

### Who It's For
- People who feel their lips look thinner or asymmetric
- Those wanting subtle, balanced enhancement (not "trout pout")
- Anyone with naturally thin upper or lower lip wanting better proportion
- People who've had filler elsewhere and want a more natural look

### Pricing
| Treatment | Price |
|---|---|
| Lip filler | £150 |
| Lip flip | £80 |
| Upper perioral filler | £150 |

### FAQs
- **How long does it last?** 6-12 months depending on product, metabolism, and area.
- **Will it hurt?** Topical numbing cream is applied. Lidocaine in the filler also helps.
- **How long is recovery?** Swelling 24-72 hours; final shape at 2 weeks.
- **Can it be reversed?** Yes — hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if needed.
- **Will it look natural?** We treat conservatively. You should look like you, refreshed.

## 11. TREATMENT PAGE — Dermal Fillers (`/treatments/dermal-fillers/`)

### Hero
- **H1:** Dermal Filler Treatment
- **Subtext:** Restore lost volume, redefine structure, refresh your appearance. Hyaluronic acid filler for cheeks, jaw, chin, and more.
- **Price:** From £280

### What It Is
Dermal filler uses hyaluronic acid to restore facial volume, redefine contours, and soften the visible signs of ageing. Where anti-wrinkle treatment relaxes muscles, filler restores structure — making both treatments complementary, not interchangeable.

### Who It's For
- People noticing volume loss in cheeks or temples
- Those wanting a more defined jawline or chin
- Anyone bothered by deepening nasolabial folds or marionette lines
- People seeking facial harmony and proportion

### FAQs
- **How long does it last?** 12-18 months on average.
- **Is it reversible?** Yes — hyaluronic acid filler is reversible.
- **Does it migrate?** Properly placed filler should stay put. Migration is associated with poor technique or excessive volume.

## 13. TREATMENT PAGE — Microneedling (`/treatments/microneedling/`)

### Hero
- **H1:** Microneedling
- **Subtext:** Stimulate your skin's renewal with collagen, PDRN, or exosomes. Improve texture, tone, fine lines, and glow.
- **Price:** From £100

### What It Is
Microneedling creates tiny, controlled micro-channels in the skin, triggering its natural repair process — and allowing powerful topical products to penetrate where they actually work. At KESHO we offer three modern boosters: collagen, PDRN, and exosomes.

### Who It's For
- People with dull, uneven skin texture
- Those with mild scarring (acne, surgical)
- Anyone wanting overall skin rejuvenation
- People who want results from their skincare to actually show

### Pricing

|---|---|---|---|
| With PDRN | £150 | £250 | £380 |
| With Collagen | £100 | £180 | £190 |

### FAQs
- **What's the difference between PDRN, collagen, and exosomes?** See [our full comparison guide](/blog/microneedling-pdrn-collagen-exosomes/).

- **How many sessions do I need?** Most clients see optimal results from a course of 3, spaced 4-6 weeks apart.
